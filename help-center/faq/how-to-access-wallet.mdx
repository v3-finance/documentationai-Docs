## Overview

You can access your wallet through the V3 Custody dashboard.

## Steps

1. Visit `https://dashboard.v3custody.com`
2. Login with your registered email and password
3. Complete Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
4. Navigate to "Wallets" from the menu
5. Click on your desired wallet to view details

<Callout kind="tip">
Use 2FA for additional security.
</Callout>
