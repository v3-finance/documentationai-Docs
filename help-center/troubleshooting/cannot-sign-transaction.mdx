---
title: Cannot Sign Transaction
description: What to do if you can't sign transactions.
---

## Symptoms

- Button to sign is unresponsive
- "Unable to reach signing service" error

## Causes

- Session expired
- Network issues
- Insufficient signing permissions

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh Session">
    Log out and log back in to the dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Network">
    Ensure stable internet connection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Permissions">
    Check that your user account has transaction signing rights in "Settings > Users".
  </Step>
</Steps>
