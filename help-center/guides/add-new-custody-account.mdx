## Overview

Follow these steps to add a new custody account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Dashboard">
    Go to `https://dashboard.v3custody.com` and log in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Accounts">
    Click on "Accounts" in the side menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Account">
    Click "Add New Account", complete the form (name, permissions), and submit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Review the details and confirm creation. The new account will appear in your accounts list.
  </Step>
</Steps>
