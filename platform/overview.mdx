The **Platform** section covers cross-cutting technical topics shared across the entire API: how to address your instance, how to read amounts, how to page and filter, and how to handle errors and webhooks. Both the [guides](/guides/overview) and the [concepts](/concepts/overview) build on these topics.

## Prerequisites

- Your instance's base URL, shown as `[BASE_URL]` in the examples.
- An access token for the `Authorization` header (see [Authentication](/authentication)).
